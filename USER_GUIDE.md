## 1. Why This Platform?

Instead of endless message loops, unread polls, and confusion across multiple WhatsApp groups, our batch has an official, dedicated reunion planner where everyone gets an equal voice.

### The Decision Rule

> 🏆 **The date with the highest number of votes becomes the official college visit date.**  
> Everyone meets on that day to collect certificates, take batch photos, and celebrate together!

## 2. How to Join (Fast & Password-Free)

No passwords, email confirmations, or tedious registration forms are required.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  1. Enter your Full Name (e.g., Priya Sharma)│
│  2. Enter 10-Digit Mobile (e.g., 9876543210) │
```

### Why Do We Ask for Your Mobile Number?

- It acts as your **unique voter ticket** so you can revisit the platform anytime to see updated counts, change your vote, or chat.
- **Your phone number is strictly private.** It is never shown to other batchmates, never displayed in chat, and never listed in the directory.

## 3. Casting & Changing Your Vote

### Eligible Dates

All available dates are strictly after November 24, 2026 (**earliest date: November 25, 2026**), in accordance with the Academic Registrar's certificate distribution schedule.

### Step-by-Step Voting

1. Tap **Vote** on the top menu (or bottom tab bar on mobile).
2. Choose your preferred view:
   - **Calendar View:** Shows dates marked in November and December 2026.
   - **List View:** Shows date cards with day of the week and vote counters.
3. Click on your preferred date (e.g., _Friday, 27 November 2026_).
4. Tap **Confirm My Vote**.
5. You'll see a green confirmation badge: _"Your vote has been recorded!"_

### Want to Change Your Vote?

Changed your mind or have a travel conflict? No problem!

- Open the **Vote** page anytime before the polling deadline.
- Select a new date and confirm.
- **Single Vote Guarantee:** Your previous vote is updated in-place without creating duplicate votes.

## 7. Privacy & Security Guarantee

We take batch privacy seriously:

- 🔒 **No Phone Number Leaks:** Your mobile number is encrypted in sessions and excluded from all public directories and chat payloads.
- 🛡️ **Anti-Tampering:** Vote counts are calculated directly in secure database stored procedures, not in the browser.
- 🚫 **One Vote Per Person:** Each mobile number is restricted to exactly one active vote per poll.

## 8. Using Like a Mobile App (PWA)

Campus Reunion Poll is designed as a mobile-first app with a fixed bottom navigation bar:

### Add to Android Home Screen (Chrome)

1. Open the website in Google Chrome on your phone.
2. Tap the three dots menu (**⋮**) in the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. The reunion icon will appear on your phone screen just like a native app!

### Add to iPhone Home Screen (Safari)

1. Open the website in Safari on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top right corner.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Can I vote for dates before November 25, 2026?

**A:** No. The college examination cell and administrative office confirmed that B.Tech degrees are printed and ready for collection only from November 25, 2026 onwards.

#### Q2: What happens if two dates tie?

**A:** If there is a tie, an alert will be displayed. The Class Representative will either initiate a runoff poll between the tied dates or announce a verified date based on department counter availability.

#### Q3: Can I change my vote after the deadline?

**A:** No. Once the deadline passes, polling is closed and the official winning date is permanently declared.

#### Q4: I forgot my login details, what should I do?

**A:** Just enter the same name and 10-digit mobile number you used initially. The platform will automatically recognize you and restore your vote and messages.
